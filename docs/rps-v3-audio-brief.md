# Audio direction and custom voice route

V3 uses legally reusable recorded human performances, not text-to-speech:
Kenney's fighting announcer, cicifyre's female efforts, qubodup's male efforts,
and nene's symphonic-metal boss theme. Source links and processing are recorded
in assets/rps/CREDITS.txt and rps-v3-audio-sources.json. The two male characters
currently share efforts. There is no connected custom voice/music generator or
funded audio API in this workspace. The shipped voice is stock rather than a
bespoke announcer; no speech is generated on the player's device.

For a unique voice, commission a voice actor with game/commercial usage rights.
Ask for close-mic dry WAV, 48 kHz/24-bit mono, three takes per line, no music or
reverb. Direction: an absurdly serious arena announcer treating rock paper
scissors as the final duel of civilization. Warm chest resonance, explosive
consonants, theatrical pauses, clear diction; vary delivery rather than shouting
every word. Do not imitate a named performer. Keep most calls under 1.5 seconds.

Suggested original lines: “Hands of destiny!”; “Choose your weapon!”; “Round
one!”; “One hand remains!”; “The arena holds its breath!”; “A draw? Again!”;
“Victory!”; “Unbelievable!”; “A legend is born!”; “The crowd demands a rematch!”
Each fighter also needs six varied attack efforts, three shocked gasps, three
frustrated cries, three relieved laughs and three short victory shouts. Keep
efforts 0.2–0.7 seconds; avoid intelligible borrowed anime dialogue.

Alternatively, audition an original designed voice with an expressive licensed
service such as ElevenLabs, then export approved clips using the same brief.
A paid plan grants commercial use subject to its terms; the free plan does not.
Check current terms before producing assets:
https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/en-us/articles/13313564601361-Can-I-publish-the-content-I-generate-on-the-platform
https://elevenlabs.io/pricing
No paid account, purchase, voice clone or external generation was performed.

For bespoke music, commission/license a fast symphonic-metal instrumental:
170–190 BPM, distorted guitar melody, double-time drums, heroic brass/strings,
a memorable rising theme, a short sting and a seamlessly looping action section.
Request stems so sudden-death tension can drop to near silence and the decisive
reveal can bring the full ensemble back. Match energy, not an existing anime tune.
