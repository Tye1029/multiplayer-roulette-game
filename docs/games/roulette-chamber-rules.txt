Authoritative six-chamber Russian Roulette rules

- Every new game creates one independent server-side chamber cycle.
- The live round position is selected with crypto.randomInt(1, 7), giving positions 1 through 6 equal probability.
- bulletPosition stores the original loaded position for the complete chamber cycle.
- remaining starts at bulletPosition and decreases by exactly one after each accepted blank shot.
- The live round fires only when remaining equals 1.
- Pulling the trigger never rerolls or replaces the chamber cycle.
- Passing the turn never rerolls or replaces the chamber cycle.
- Only an accepted Spin action creates a new random 1-through-6 chamber cycle.
- Spin remains limited to one accepted use per player per game.
- Hidden chamber fields are never returned to either player.
- Independent random games may legitimately repeat the same position, including position 1.
