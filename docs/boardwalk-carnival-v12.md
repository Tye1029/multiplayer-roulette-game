# Boardwalk Carnival V12

Mountain Climber keeps its live directional controls and four physical ledge prompts, but removes the duplicate central prompt strip. Newly received authoritative prompts replenish pending input previews. Badge materials are reused. The last climb reaches the summit surface, turns the character toward the camera, and the base has a grassy meadow.

Sunset Putt selects three distinct layouts from nine on the server. Everyone plays the same selection in the same order; reconnects retain it. Six existing legacy layouts are adapted into the Party course pool without modifying legacy sources. The hippo is replaced with a marked loading pad and covered entrance leading into a clear ball chute. The covered section matches the original physics' hidden transport segment. Older active rooms retain their original course selection.

Harbor Haul has eight bite opportunities in a minute, 180 ms ripple entrances, a brighter bite flash and a dipping bobber. An early tug gives feedback without consuming the upcoming bite; successful catches and scores remain server-owned and cannot be repeated. Both ripple diameter and landed fish length are proportional to the authoritative size. The measuring and results phases preserve the fishing camera, including on mobile.

Afterglow adds softer pad depression/glow transitions, directional body lean and layered note/chime/clap sounds. Reverse-pattern rules remain unchanged.

Lantern Landing uses directed, articulated jumps for every platform pair. A jump launched toward the final card's color before the bell succeeds even if still airborne when the other platforms drop. The second card on fake-out rounds is always authoritative; choosing the decoy still eliminates the player.

Tidal Hop resolves on physical contact, rather than 180 ms later. The client can show the corresponding collision pose immediately using the public obstacle trajectory; it cannot award points or alter outcomes. Every clean jump earns 300 points. Hits enforce 2.5 seconds of recovery on the server, with a stagger and orbiting stars. Log spacing leaves time to recover before the next required jump. Three hits still end the run.

Validation includes randomized course deals, matching course physics through all twelve golf turns, premature-reel recovery, all fake-out outcomes, jump/stun timing, all twelve directed platform jumps, desktop/mobile renders of all nine courses, stable fishing cameras, and summit placement. Existing Party regression tests and the build's protected-game checks remain enabled.

Superseded central-arrow markup, rendering code, CSS and animation rules were removed. Legacy game media and historical documentation remain because the separate original games and validation history still use them. Marker: `BOARDWALK_CARNIVAL_V12`.
