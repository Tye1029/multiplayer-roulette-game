Midnight Run recorded weather

rain-soft.ogg and thunder-distant.ogg are edited excerpts of:
Gentle Rain Mix (2018), esh9419, 2018-02-06
https://freesound.org/people/esh9419/sounds/417616/
Licensed CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The creator identifies source recordings as their Rainstorm V2 and
Rain heavy 1 (rural) by jmbphilmes, both CC0.
Downloaded from the public high-quality preview:
https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/417/417616_3285887-hq.mp3

Edits: rain excerpt at 60-84 seconds, 100 Hz high-pass and 3400 Hz
low-pass, gain adjustment and one-second seam crossfade (23-second loop).
Thunder excerpt at 34-48 seconds, 45 Hz high-pass and 950 Hz low-pass,
gain adjustment and softened attack/four-second release.
Both resampled to stereo 32 kHz and encoded Ogg Vorbis quality 3.
Playback gain is deliberately low and independent of gameplay outcomes.
