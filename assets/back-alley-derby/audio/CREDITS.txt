https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds
CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Trimmed to one shot, filtered, peak-limited and faded for a quiet starting cue.

crowd.ogg and cheer.ogg: "Free Crowd Cheering Sounds" by Gregor Quendel.
https://opengameart.org/content/free-crowd-cheering-sounds
https://www.gregorquendel.com/
CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Adapted from the existing licensed site excerpts of 07 (Soft cheering and
chatter) and 04 (Strong cheering II - Short). Filtered, softened and converted
to mono OGG for a distant alley audience. No endorsement implied.

step-1.ogg, step-2.ogg, step-3.ogg: Kenney Impact Sounds, CC0.
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
Full original license is in Kenney-LICENSE.txt. Runtime pitch, filtering and
stereo variation create small paw scurries from the recorded textures.

The quiet pizzicato music and tonal countdown/result cues are original
Web Audio compositions for Xan Duels. All game audio respects shared mute,
starts only after interaction, and stops when the page is hidden.
