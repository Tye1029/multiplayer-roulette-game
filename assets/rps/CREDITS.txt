HAND OF DOOM — asset credits (V3, October 9, 2026)

Music: "Boss Battle #2 [Symphonic Metal]" by nene, CC0.
https://opengameart.org/content/boss-battle-2-symphonic-metal
Guitars, strings, brass and drums. Normalized and compressed to MP3.
The source pack's names are reversed (as noted by its author): opening.wav
is our 26.48-second loop; loop.wav is our 39.72-second introduction.

Announcer: "Voiceover pack: Fighter (40+ taunts)" by Kenney, CC0.
https://opengameart.org/content/voiceover-pack-fighter-40-taunts
Recorded human performance. Thirteen calls, trimmed, normalized, light stadium
echo, mono MP3. This is a licensed stock announcer, not a custom character voice.

Lyra and Bryn: "Female RPG Voice Starter Pack" by cicifyre, CC0.
https://opengameart.org/content/female-rpg-voice-starter-pack
Voice types 2 and 3 respectively; three attacks and three hurt reactions each.
Maximus and Voss: "15 vocal male strain/hurt/pain/jump sounds" by qubodup, CC0.
https://opengameart.org/content/15-vocal-male-strainhurtpainjump-sounds
Three attacks and three reactions shared by the male fighters. Trimmed,
normalized and compressed to mono MP3; no imitation of an existing anime cast.
CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Crowd: "Free Crowd Cheering Sounds" by Gregor Quendel, CC BY 4.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/free-crowd-cheering-sounds
https://www.gregorquendel.com/
Excerpts of 07 (Soft cheering and chatter) and 04 (Strong cheering II - Short),
fades and MP3 recompression. No endorsement implied.

Reveal impact contains a short excerpt of "Metalmania" by Kevin MacLeod
(incompetech.com), CC BY 4.0, mixed with a pitched swish.
https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1700023
CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The old Metalmania background loop is removed; this impact remains in use.

Swish and cut: "Swishes Sound Pack" by artisticdude for Summoning Wars, CC0.
https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack
swish-7, echo, MP3 compression; cut is a short faded excerpt.

Arena and characters: built-in OpenAI image generation, October 9, 2026.
Four distinct gladiators: Maximus, Voss, Lyra and Bryn. Each has nine illustrated
square panels: look-back, eyes, rage, tears, rock, paper, scissors, victory,
disbelief. Atlas cells preserve proportions; opponent art is mirrored inward.
Stone fist, flaming paper palm and blade scissors are generated transparent PNGs.
Full body originals remain used for the roster and concealed-hand staging.
Prompt provenance: docs/rps-art-prompts.txt, docs/rps-v2-art-prompts.txt,
docs/rps-v3-art-prompts.txt. Audio transformations: docs/rps-v3-audio-sources.json.

All media loads only on this game's page. Audio starts after a user gesture.
Superseded synthesized voice clips and the old background music were removed.
