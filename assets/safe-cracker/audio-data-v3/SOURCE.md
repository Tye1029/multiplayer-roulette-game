# Safe Cracker dial audio v24

The user-supplied metallic recordings remain archived in this folder, but the native MP3/data-URI route was removed from active dial playback because it remained silent on the target Android browser despite successful static and deployment checks.

Active dial route:
- Builds a synchronous PCM steel-ratchet buffer inside the browser's existing WebAudio context.
- Uses a sharp impact, three damped steel resonances, a lower lock-body knock, a pawl catch, and a small rebound click.
- Creates the buffer without network loading, media elements, MP3 decoding, cache reuse, or asynchronous sample preparation.
- Unlocks WebAudio during pointer, touch, or keyboard interaction and starts each detent immediately through the active audio destination.
- Uses one consistent mechanical sound at fixed pitch, with no arcade melody or musical oscillator sequence.

The background ambience remains the smooth 21-second vault-room tone. Correct-number, incorrect-number, intro, and vault-opening cues remain separate and unchanged.
