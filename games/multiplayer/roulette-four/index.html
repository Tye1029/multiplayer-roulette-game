The table rules

  1. Everyone contributes to the pot. The opening spin picks the first player; turns move clockwise.
  2. You must shoot at least once on your turn. After a safe shot, shoot again or pass.
  3. The first shot starts at 20 Chips and rises by 20 Chips after every safe shot, even across passes.
  4. Each player has one spin per game. Spin before shooting or before passing. It randomizes the bullet location and resets the next shot to 20 Chips.
  5. The first elimination by a live shot ends the game. Whoever was eliminated loses their Chips, which are returned to the pot for the survivors to split. Survivors keep their banked Chips. (Survivors who pulled the trigger more get a slightly bigger cut of the pot.)
  6. An empty pot also ends the game.

Each turn has a 60-second timer. If it expires, that player is eliminated and their banked Chips are split equally among the remaining players. The game continues, skipping eliminated players. If only one player remains, they claim the remaining pot.
