    The table rules

    1. Everyone contributes $100. The opening spin picks the first player; turns move clockwise.
    2. You must shoot at least once on your turn. After a safe shot, shoot again or pass.
    3. The next shot starts at $20 and rises by $20 after every safe shot, even across passes.
    4. Each player has one spin per round. It randomizes the bullet and resets the next shot to $20. Shoot before passing after a spin.
    5. The first elimination ends the round. The eliminated bank returns to the pot. Survivors keep their banks and split what remains by safe-shot rank: 25:30:35 weights (27.78%, 33.33%, 38.89%). Ties share their ranks equally.
    6. An empty pot also ends the round. Every cent is paid out; there is no house deduction.
    7. All four players must accept the rematch within 10 seconds. A rematch resets banks, spins and shot counts, with a fresh turn order and bot personalities.
