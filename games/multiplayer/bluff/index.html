THE ART OF GETTING AWAY WITH IT

How to play

  1. Each player receives 10 random cards: crowns, coins, and skulls.
  2. Each round names a required symbol. Both players secretly select one or more cards, claiming they all match. Lock in within 30 seconds or automatically lose.
  3. Once both lock in, see how many cards your rival played. Choose Believe or Call Bluff. You have 20 seconds; no decision defaults to Believe.
  4. Every card is revealed. If believed, the play is discarded—even if fake. A caught bluffer takes their cards back. A false accuser takes all the opponent’s genuine played cards.
  5. Both plays resolve together. Empty your hand after all transfers to win. If both hands are empty, it’s a draw. Both missing the play deadline is also a draw.

Remember the cards that return to each hand. The next big play might be honest… or a very convincing lie.
