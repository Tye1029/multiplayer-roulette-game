THE 30-SECOND GUIDE

Roll. Hold. Score.

  1. Roll up to three times. Tap any dice to keep them. Roll the rest, or score early.
  2. Choose one empty category. Preview the points, then bank your score. Each category can be used once, including a zero.
  3. Highest total wins. Everyone gets 13 turns. Reach 63 in Ones–Sixes for a 35-point bonus.

Five matching dice score 50 in Yahtzee. Further Yahtzees earn +100 if you already scored 50 there. Joker rules require the matching upper category first, then any open lower category. Eligible scores are highlighted automatically.

Play in turns with 2–4 friends. Reloading reconnects you on this browser. Leaving an active game ends it for everyone.
