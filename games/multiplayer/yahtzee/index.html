How to play

  1. Both players get 30 seconds per round. Roll five dice, click the ones you want to keep, and reroll the rest up to twice.
  2. The scorecard previews your available points after every roll. Click a category to bank it. Each category can be used once; blank previews mean zero.
  3. Reach 63 in Ones–Sixes for a 35-point bonus. Five matching dice score 50 in Yahtzee. Further Yahtzees give +100 if your Yahtzee box already holds 50; Joker placement is enforced.
  4. The next round begins when both players bank, or when time expires. On timeout, your best available score is banked automatically. Without a roll, an empty category gets zero.
  5. Highest total after 13 rounds wins. You can watch the opponent’s dice and scores live.
